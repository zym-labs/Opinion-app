# Opinion Community Guidelines — DRAFT

Opinion works when people give honest, useful reasons. Keep it that way.

**Do**
- Ask real questions you want help deciding.
- Give a reason that would help the asker: what you know, what you'd do, why.
- Stay on the question, not the person.
- In **Relationships**, describe the situation, not the person: no names, screenshots or details that would let anyone recognise them.

**Don't**
- Post anything that identifies, targets or rates a real private person (names, photos, @handles, "which of my classmates…", "is the guy in seat 12 annoying?"). Questions like this are blocked automatically.
- Share personal information — yours or anyone else's (phone numbers, addresses, emails).
- Post hate, harassment, threats, sexual content, or content involving minors.
- Encourage self-harm, dangerous challenges, or illegal activity.
- Spam, advertise, or coordinate votes.
- Try to trick the AI summary (e.g. "ignore previous instructions…").
- Claim expertise to mislead — categories are self-selected, not verified.

**Sponsored questions** follow the same rules, are always labelled, and only appear for people who turned them on.

**What happens**
Content is checked automatically before it appears and can be reported by anyone. Breaking these rules can lead to removal, a warning, suspension or a permanent ban. The person affected is told which rule applied and can appeal in the app; a person reviews every appeal. We publish aggregate figures in our [transparency report](/transparency).

**If you're struggling**
Opinion isn't the place to decide whether you're safe. If a question or reason mentions self-harm, we show crisis helplines (including your campus support line if your university has added one) instead of posting it. If you or someone else is in danger, contact local emergency services.
