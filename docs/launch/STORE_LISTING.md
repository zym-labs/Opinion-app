**Description:**

Can’t decide? Ask people who actually know.

Opinion lets you post a quick two-option poll to people in the topics you pick — Tech, Career, Money, Fashion and more — or to a community you belong to, like your campus. Everyone who votes says why.

When your poll closes, you get:
• the result, in percentages
• an AI summary of the main reasons on both sides — including the minority view
• up to three featured reasons, shared anonymously by voters who agreed

Private by default
• No followers, no likes, no public profiles
• Nobody sees who voted or who wrote what
• Only you can share your poll’s result

Fair by design
• Results stay hidden until the poll closes, so early votes don’t sway later ones
• Vote on others’ polls to earn your own — everyone who asks also helps
• Every poll and reason is checked before it appears, and anyone can report

Polls run for 3 to 24 hours. Opinion is for adults 18+. AI summaries can be wrong; results are opinions, not professional advice.
