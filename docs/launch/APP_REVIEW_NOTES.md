# App Review notes (paste into App Store Connect → App Review Information)

> Fill in the demo account before submitting. Keep it onboarded, in the Tech category, with poll credits and at least one completed poll.

**Demo account:** `[review@domain]` — sign in with "Continue with email". The review account receives the fixed code `[code]` (configured as a test OTP for this address only).

**What Opinion is.** Opinion is an anonymous *polling* app for adults (18+). A user asks a two-option question to people in chosen interest categories or a community. Voters pick an option and give a short reason. When the poll closes, results are shown as percentages with an AI-written summary of the reasons. It is **not** a chat or messaging app: there are no direct messages, replies, comments, followers or public profiles, and no user can see who voted or who wrote anything.

**User-generated content safeguards (Guideline 1.2):**
- *Filtering:* every question, option, image and reason is checked by an automated moderation service before it is saved or shown. New accounts get stricter thresholds.
- *Reporting:* every poll and every featured quote has a Report action (poll screen → "Report"; result screen → "Report" under a quote). Reports are reviewed by our moderators within 24 hours.
- *Blocking:* after reporting a poll, users can hide all polls from that creator ("Also hide polls from this creator"); manage in Settings → Hidden creators.
- *Contact:* Settings → Help & contact, and [support URL].
- *Moderation tools:* moderators can remove content and suspend accounts; authors are told which rule was broken.

**Age (1.2.1, age rating):** the app is rated 18+. Sign-up asks for birth year and uses the platform age-range signal; under-18s cannot create an account.

**Sign in with Apple (4.8):** offered alongside Google and email.

**Account deletion (5.1.1(v)):** Profile → Settings → Delete account (type DELETE to confirm). Deletion is immediate.

**AI:** summaries are labelled "AI-generated from voters' reasons. May be inaccurate." Sensitive categories (Medicine, Law, Money) add "Crowd opinions, not professional advice."

**How to see a result quickly:** the demo account has a completed poll in My Polls (permanent result with AI summary) and a voted poll whose result appears under "Results ready" in the Feed (each voter can view a result once).
