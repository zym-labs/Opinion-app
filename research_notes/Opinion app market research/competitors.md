# Competitive Landscape for "Opinion" (temporary 2-option expert polls + written reasons + AI summary)

Research date: 2026-10-02. Each fact is dated where the source gives a date. About 14 tool calls were used, so coverage is broad but shallow. Many small apps the brief names could not be verified (see Gaps).

## 1. Crowd-decision / poll apps: features, traction, funding, status

### Takeaway
Consumer "ask the crowd" poll apps have a long history (2010–2017). They follow the same pattern: fast early growth from novelty or teen virality, then decline, acquisition or a pivot. None of them combined targeted expert audiences, written reasons and AI synthesis. The surviving poll products are mostly B2B or event tools (Slido, Poll Everywhere) or messaging add-ons (Polls for iMessage).

### Cited Findings
- **Seesaw (decision app, 2013)**: iPhone app from the CoTweet founders. Users posted up to 4 photo options to their phone contacts or Twitter/Facebook, and friends tapped to vote. Its network was built on close friends from the address book. — [AllThingsD, 2013-02-07](http://allthingsd.com/20130207/seesaw-decision-making-app-helps-ask-friends-for-opinions/); [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/cotweet-founders-launch-seesaw-for-iphone-a-decision-making-app-that-gets-your-friends-opinion-when-you-need-it). Note: "Seesaw" today is an unrelated school-portfolio company. — [TechCrunch 2018](https://techcrunch.com/2018/02/07/seesaw-for-schools/)
- **Pop (2014)**: users took two photos and polled friends. — [TechCrunch, 2014-02-26](https://techcrunch.com/2014/02/26/need-a-quick-answer-pop-lets-you-take-two-photos-poll-your-friends/). A different app named "Pop! – Polls with Friends" (group polls with chat and live results) is on the App Store now. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/pop-polls-with-friends/id6755626759)
- **Thumb (formerly Opinionaided, launched June 2010)**: a social opinion app that started as social-shopping "try on and poll" and became general purpose. It claimed about 5 hours of average engagement per month in 2012. It was acquired by YPulse, a youth market-research firm, so its endpoint was a research business rather than a social network. — [VentureBeat 2011](https://venturebeat.com/2011/12/08/opinionaided-rebrands-as-thumb-targets); [TechCrunch 2012-07-31](https://techcrunch.com/2012/07/31/thumb-android-3-0/); [MrWeb](https://www.mrweb.com/drno/news17486.htm); [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com/organization/opinionaided)
- **Polar**: either/or polls. It raised $1.2M and claimed about 50 votes per user per day, against about 6 "thumbs" per user per day on Thumb. Google acquired it for Google+. — [BetaKit](https://betakit.com/polar-adds-1-2m-for-social-voting-app-sees-almost-6m-votes-in-3-months/); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/?p=1057081)
- **Wishbone (2015, from the Science Inc. incubator)**: a "compare anything" 2-option app for teens. It reached 3.1M MAU within about 4 months, and later 5M MAU, 91% female and mostly aged 13–25. Users cast about 25M votes per day. It made money from brand-sponsored polls and was hacked in March 2017. — [TechTimes 2015-10-06](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/91718/20151006/wishbone-social-barometer-app-skyrockets-among-teens-3-million-plus-users-in-just-four-months.htm); [AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/mobile/teen-polling-app-wishbone-intersection-advertising-content/); [Digiday](https://digiday.com/marketing/brands-stump-teen-votes-wishbone/); [TechCrunch 2017-03-15](https://techcrunch.com/2017/03/15/teen-quiz-app-wishbone-hacked-users-emails-and-phone-numbers-exposed/?ncid=rss)
- **Jelly (Biz Stone, January 2014)**: a visual Q&A app. It had heavy launch downloads, then active users "spiraled down" because most people did not want to ask questions while answers were plentiful. It was paused in November 2014 for an opinion app called Super, then "unpivoted" back to Jelly in 2016. — [Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/225138/twitter-cofounder-biz-stone-on-jelly-s-spectacular-failure-and-the-yet-to-be-determined-success-of.html); [TechCrunch 2016-01-07](https://techcrunch.com/2016/01/07/unpivot)
- **tbh (2017) and Gas (2022, Nikita Bier)**: anonymous, compliment-only polls for high schoolers. Meta acquired tbh. Gas hit No. 1 on the US App Store with 10M+ downloads and about $11M revenue in about 3 months. Discord bought it in January 2023 (about $50M reported) and shut it down in November 2023. — [Wikipedia: Gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gas_(app)); [TechCrunch 2023-01-17](https://techcrunch.com/2023/01/17/discord-acquires-gas-a-compliments-based-social-media-app-for-teens); [The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/discord-shutters-gas-making-it-two-in-a-row-for-gas-founder). As of July 2025, Bier leads product at X. — [SF Standard, 2025-07-02](https://sfstandard.com/2025/07/02/nikita-bier-leads-product-x/)
- **Polls Platform / Polls for iMessage (Phoenix, founded 2019)**: about $1.17M raised over 2 rounds, the first in August 2021. — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/pollsplatform/__qZlNqY6hxhg3ac4zLANmxjGDnvap3Wftx5BHDvm-8EA); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/polls-for-imessage/id1142329922)
- **B2B and event polling**: Poll Everywhere 2.0 shipped in August 2025 with AI-assisted poll creation and anonymous or verified participant modes. Slido is owned by Cisco/Webex. — [Poll Everywhere](https://landing.polleverywhere.com/poll-everywhere-2025-roadmap); [SurveySparrow](https://surveysparrow.com/blog/best-poll-apps/)
- **Blippy**, an adjacent social-review app, shut down in May 2011 with about 100K registered users, only 30% of whom ever shared anything. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blippy)

### Inferences
- Two-option polls get a lot of engagement: Polar averaged 50 votes per user per day and Wishbone saw 25M votes per day. But votes alone did not keep users or produce a business. The exits were acqui-hires (Polar to Google, Thumb to YPulse) or brand-sponsored content (Wishbone).
- Opinion's written-reason requirement adds friction that the high-volume apps avoided. That could cut vote volume but raise answer quality. This is a deliberate trade-off.

### Gaps
- Deciderr, Choicer, Hint, Wiser, OneVote, Swayy: no reliable data found (downloads, ratings, funding). These may be very small or mislabeled. They need to be checked directly on App Store, Play or appfigures.
- Strawpoll and Polly (Slack poll bot) traction were not researched.
- No Sensor Tower or appfigures download estimates were found for any poll app in 2024–2026.
- No well-funded consumer 2-option decision app launched in 2024–2026 turned up in search.

## 2. Adjacent: Reddit, anonymous/community apps, AI advice, prediction markets

### Takeaway
Free, unstructured alternatives already serve the demand at large scale: Reddit judgment subreddits (AITA has 24.5M members) and ChatGPT, where "making decisions" is about 8.5% of messages. Anonymous campus and workplace apps show that trusted, verified communities can be funded, but teen anonymity brings regulatory risk. Prediction markets show that money-backed aggregation is where opinion-aggregation capital is going.

### Cited Findings
- **r/AmItheAsshole**: 24.5M members as of 2026-08-17, created in 2013. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R/AmItheAsshole); see also [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/radio/sunday/reddit-community-1.7059122)
- **ChatGPT**: about 73% of conversations are non-work. "Making Decisions and Solving Problems" is 8.5% of messages and "Consultation/Advice" is 9.2% (OpenAI/NBER study, 2025). — [OpenAI paper](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/a253471f-8260-40c6-a2cc-aa93fe9f142e/economic-research-chatgpt-usage-paper.pdf). 34% of US adults had used ChatGPT as of June 2025. — [Pew, 2025-06-25](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/06/25/34-of-us-adults-have-used-chatgpt-about-double-the-share-in-2023/)
- **Fizz (anonymous college social)**: $41.5M raised over 3 rounds (investors include NEA and Owl), live at 300+ colleges. In early 2025 it reported DAU up 61% and daily posts up 245% year to date, and it is building a marketplace. — [TechCrunch, 2025-02-06](https://techcrunch.com/2025/02/06/fizz-brings-on-tiktok-alum-to-help-build-out-its-marketplace-and-recommendation-engine/); [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/fizz-social/__mbUB8ZfUv_1-USbOsvAjRpThIwgcWPg7VXu65SrH1Do)
- **Yik Yak**: shut down after bullying problems and an acqui-hire by Square, relaunched in 2021, then acquired by Sidechat in March 2023. Users were migrated and their karma was wiped. — [TechCrunch, 2023-03-16](https://techcrunch.com/2023/03/16/anonymous-app-sidechat-picks-up-rival-yik-yak-and-users-arent-happy/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yik_Yak)
- **NGL**: in July 2024 it settled with the FTC and the LA District Attorney for $5M. The company was accused of sending fake messages to boost engagement and of deceptive claims about AI moderation, and it was banned from serving users under 18. This was the first FTC order requiring an age block. — [FTC, July 2024](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/07/ftc-order-will-ban-ngl-labs-its-founders-offering-anonymous-messaging-apps-kids-under-18-halt)
- **Blind (Teamblind)**: workplace community verified by work email. $74.9M raised over 7 rounds, latest Series C on 2025-07-07, 5M+ professionals. — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/teamblind/__MrO4gNXVV5InE3po33SuUzF0wqD6Qhdg1uMpHSaOxr4)
- **Jodel** (Berlin, 2014): about $6.76M raised. — [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/jodel/__rpWwPTUb-o_6sOwdAyY-Bwu9n2u_TirMouB9LPsKzB4)
- **Prediction markets**:
  - Kalshi was valued at about $11B in late 2025, raised $1B at $22B (led by Coatue) in 2026, and was reported seeking about $40B in June 2026. — [CoinDesk, 2026-03-07](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/03/07/kalshi-polymarket-seeking-usd20-billion-valuations-in-fundraising-talks-wsj); [CoinDesk, 2026-06-24](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/06/24/kalshi-targets-a-massive-usd40-billion-valuation-widening-lead-over-rival-polymarket)
  - Polymarket raised about $1B at about $15B (April 2026) and was reported in talks at $20B in August 2026. — [DeFi Rate](https://defirate.com/news/polymarket-eyes-15b-valuation-gap-with-kalshi-widens/); [Coin Republic, 2026-08-05](https://www.thecoinrepublic.com/2026/08/05/can-polymarket-overtake-kalshi-20b-valuation-talks-begin/)
  - The two platforms passed $150B combined lifetime volume in April 2026 (per the search summary; not verified against a primary source).

### Inferences
- Opinion competes most directly with posting on AITA or relationship_advice, or asking ChatGPT. It needs to beat Reddit on speed, privacy and audience targeting, and beat ChatGPT on real human judgment from relevant people.
- Verified communities (Blind's email check, Fizz's .edu check) are proven, fundable wedges. Opinion's "curated communities" could borrow that verification model.
- Avoid teen-anonymous positioning because of the NGL precedent and the Gas and Yik Yak churn. An 18+ age gate and honest engagement mechanics (no fake prompts) should be built in from the start.

### Gaps
- Member counts for r/relationship_advice and r/whatshouldido were not retrieved. Quora's current traffic was not retrieved. No current DAU figures for Sidechat or Yik Yak.
- Manifold was not researched. Character-style AI-advice apps were not researched.

## 3. Why Seesaw-style crowd-decision apps failed or stayed small; growth loops that worked

### Takeaway
The failures had four documented causes: an imbalance of askers to answerers, novelty decay, reliance on the asker's small friend graph, and harassment or regulatory problems for anonymous teen apps. The growth loops that worked were school-by-school teen virality with polls about each other (tbh, Gas), brand-sponsored content (Wishbone) and verified-community seeding (Fizz, Blind).

### Cited Findings
- Demand imbalance: at Jelly, "most people didn't want to ask questions"; answerers outnumbered askers and active users collapsed after the launch spike. — [Macworld](https://www.macworld.com/article/225138/twitter-cofounder-biz-stone-on-jelly-s-spectacular-failure-and-the-yet-to-be-determined-success-of.html)
- Weak engagement: Blippy had only 30% of users ever share anything. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blippy)
- Viral but short-lived: Gas reached 10M+ downloads and about $11M in 3 months, then was shut down within 10 months of acquisition. — [Wikipedia: Gas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gas_(app)); [The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/discord-shutters-gas-making-it-two-in-a-row-for-gas-founder)
- Trust and safety: Yik Yak's bullying led to its collapse, and NGL's fake messages led to FTC action. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/03/16/anonymous-app-sidechat-picks-up-rival-yik-yak-and-users-arent-happy/); [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/07/ftc-order-will-ban-ngl-labs-its-founders-offering-anonymous-messaging-apps-kids-under-18-halt)
- Monetization paths seen so far: brand polls (Wishbone), market-research exit (Thumb to YPulse), weekly subscriptions for "who sent this" reveals (NGL; Gas earned about $11M). — [AdExchanger](https://www.adexchanger.com/mobile/teen-polling-app-wishbone-intersection-advertising-content/); [FTC consumer alert](https://consumer.ftc.gov/consumer-alerts/2024/07/anonymous-messaging-app-ngl-barred-marketing-kids-must-refund-unauthorized-charges)

### Inferences
- Seesaw's friend-graph design limited each poll's audience to a handful of contacts, which is the "small friend graph" problem. Opinion's model (strangers who chose a topic, answering in exchange for credits or reputation) fixes that, but it brings back Jelly's problem: answer supply and demand have to be balanced. Some mechanism such as "vote on N polls to post 1" is likely needed. This is inference; I found no direct Seesaw shutdown postmortem.
- Having no followers or likes removes the social-status loop that drove tbh and Gas. Opinion's loop then has to come from usefulness: sharing the AI summary card outside the app, and answerers seeing outcomes and how their expert reasoning compared.

### Gaps
- I found no published postmortem with retention data for Seesaw (2013) and could not confirm when it shut down.
- No D1/D30 retention benchmarks for poll apps were found.

## 4. Remaining gap for Opinion

### Takeaway
No verified product combines all of these: (a) audience targeting by self-selected expertise or community, (b) a required short written reason, (c) a 3–24h expiry, (d) an AI summary of majority and minority reasoning, and (e) privacy with no social graph. Reddit offers (a) loosely and (b) at length, but it is public and slow. ChatGPT offers synthesis without real people. The old poll apps offered fast votes without reasons or targeting.

### Cited Findings
- AITA's 24.5M members and ChatGPT's 8.5% decision-message share show the demand. — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R/AmItheAsshole); [OpenAI paper](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/a253471f-8260-40c6-a2cc-aa93fe9f142e/economic-research-chatgpt-usage-paper.pdf)
- Funding has gone to verified or anonymous communities (Fizz $41.5M, Blind $74.9M) and prediction markets ($15B–$40B valuations), not to consumer poll apps (Polls Platform about $1.17M). — sources above.

### Inferences
- The defensible angle for Opinion is "human, relevant, reasoned, fast, private." The AI summary is the main difference from 2013-era apps, and the written reasons are what give the AI something to summarize.
- Key risks: cold-start answer supply per category, low reason quality and spam (moderation), and being commoditized if Reddit or ChatGPT add similar features.

### Gaps
- No direct 2024–2026 competitor with this exact feature set was found. Absence in search is not proof that none exists, so an App Store and Product Hunt sweep is recommended.
